Lavapies_19

Architectural Project in Madrid by Pablo Calderón

Pablo Calderón, a young architect from Seville, approached this Lavapiés apartment renovation with the precision of someone who understands both structure and light. The project reveals itself through a careful choreography of colour — warm yellos, deep blues, and raw whites that respond to the neighbourhood's layered character. Clean lines define each space without coldness; geometry here is generous. Natural light, entering through south-facing windows, shifts across plastered walls throughout the day, turning the apartment into something almost painterly. This photographic series traces that dialogue between material, colour, and sun — documenting not just a renovation, but a considered architectural act..
