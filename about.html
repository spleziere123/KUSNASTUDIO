René Kusnawijaya +

René Kusnawijaya is an interdisciplinary designer and photographer specializing in adaptive reuse and sustainable architecture. His work focuses on building within existing structures, combining material innovation with cultural continuity to create resource-efficient, context-sensitive designs.

René Kusnawijaya academic education spans three European institutions: the Royal College of Art (RCA) in London, the ETSAS in Seville, and the Technical University of Munich (TUM).

His practice prioritizes sustainable material systems, favoring locally sourced, low-impact materials like reclaimed wood, bio-based composites, and recycled aggregates. By integrating circular economy principles, The Studios projects aim to minimize waste and energy use through modular, reversible construction techniques.

Beyond design, photography is used to document architectural processes, employing wide-angle lenses to capture spatial narratives. His professional experience includes collaborations in Munich, Bali, and Madrid, working with emerging architecture firms on adaptive reuse and interior transformation projects.

Currently based in Madrid as an autónomo, René Kusnawijaya merges research, design, and visual storytelling to advocate for architecture that is responsive to context, climate, and community.
